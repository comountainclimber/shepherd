One task. Eight steps. Everyone knows where they’re going.Real app · Sample project
01 ──

Make a plan worth following.

Organize projects into tasks. Write steps in the outline, assign Codex, Claude Code or yourself, and say what “Done when” means.

02 ──

Let the flock get to work.

Run steps in order or side by side. Each task gets its own branch and folder. Follow progress notes and changes in the timeline.

03 ──

“Done” deserves a check.

Run checks. Review work against the step. See what passed before moving on to merge requests. A confident agent isn’t a test suite.

FREE RANGE. LOCAL PASTURE.

Your computer.
Your flock. Full stop.

Shepherd runs the real Codex and Claude Code with your own logins, repositories, connectors and skills. Keep the setup you already trust.

Nothing goes to a Shepherd server. There isn’t one. No Shepherd accounts, hosted database or telemetry. Your agents still connect to their own services.
